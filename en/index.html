08 / About us

Born in Puglia,
for neighbourhood stores.

Re·Tail was born in Andria, from an idea by Fabio Di Giovanni: letting people who are looking for an item know that the store around the corner already has it, in their size.

We are not an online shop and we don't sell products. We put online what stores really have on their rails, and bring people in to try it on in person. We are starting in Bari and Andria, one neighbourhood at a time.

  • The store comes firstThe customer buys from you, at your till. No commission on sales.

  • Clear sizesEvery size is written the same way in every store, shoes always in EU sizes.
09 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Re·Tail?

Re·Tail shows you which physical stores near you have the item you are looking for, in your size, right now. You book a visit, go and try it on and, if you like it, buy it directly in the store.

How much does it cost?

Nothing: using Re·Tail and booking a visit is free. You only pay in the store, at the store's price, and only if you decide to buy.

Is the item kept aside for me?

You book the visit, not the item: the store knows you will come to try it on, but the item stays on sale until you arrive and could be sold in the meantime. The booking lasts until the end of the time slot you chose, then it expires on its own.

How do I book a visit?

Open the item, choose your size and the store, then the time slot. You get a code (RT-…) with its QR, to show at the till when you arrive. You can move, change or cancel the booking whenever you like.

Is availability up to date?

Availability comes from the stores and is refreshed every time you open Re·Tail or make a booking. An item can still be sold in the meantime: if you want to be sure, write to the store in the chat before you go.

Can I use it on my computer?

Yes: Re·Tail works in the browser, on your computer and your phone, at app.re-tail.it. The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon.

Where does it work?

We are starting in Bari and Andria, one neighbourhood at a time. You can search from any Italian town, with your location or by name, and see the Re·Tail stores within the radius you choose.

I have a store: how much does it cost?

Today Re·Tail is free for stores and takes no commission on sales. The customer buys from you, at your till, as always. It is all written in the terms for stores (in Italian).

I have a store: how do I join?

Write to us at info@re-tail.it. We activate the owner's first sign-in, then the owner invites the team from the store area.

Does Re·Tail use cookies or track me?

No. The website uses no cookies or analytics, and the app has no advertising or third-party tracking tools. We do not sell your data: the details are in the privacy notice (in Italian).

More questions? You will find them all on the platform, or write to us at info@re-tail.it.
